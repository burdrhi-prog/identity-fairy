# Identity Fairy — Product Vision

## Mission
Help people understand and organize their digital identity and account-security information with clear, compassionate explanations that preserve professional-grade evidence.

## Audience
People reviewing account security, device registrations, recovery options, and provider activity who need clarity without alarmist claims.

## User problem
Provider records are fragmented, technical, and sometimes ambiguous. People may struggle to distinguish an unfamiliar label from a verified unauthorized event.

## Product principles
- Explain the observation before offering an interpretation.
- Never equate a logical device registration with a distinct physical device or person.
- Make uncertainty visible and actionable.
- Keep original timestamps, identifiers, and source provenance traceable.
- Offer plain-language explanations without minimizing concerns.
- Default to privacy, user control, and least-privilege access.
- Design for accessible, low-cognitive-load workflows.

## Initial product hypothesis
A local-first tool that imports **user-provided** sample records and displays a source-linked timeline may help people review account activity more clearly. This is a hypothesis to test, not a validated outcome.

## Non-goals for the first release
No live account monitoring, credential collection, automatic breach determination, attribution of an operator, or promises of identity protection.
