# Identity Fairy Home — Original Ambient Hardware Companion

**Status:** product and hardware concept; no physical prototype built. Inspiration: user's recollection of a physical USB i-Buddy with moving wings and a removable monitor attachment. This is historical design research, **not** a proposed copy or affiliation with MSN, Microsoft or i-Buddy.

## Product promise
A small, removable, quiet companion on a monitor that communicates important *authorized, observable* account changes while the owner gets on with their life. The local-first tablet/desktop app provides context, review, timestamps and source links. No claim of continuous coverage or guaranteed account safety.

## Hardware concept
- **Original, modern characters:** Identity Fairy and an independently designed Desktop Dude. No copied silhouette, logos, icon files, brand colours, branding or software assets.
- **Shared electronics:** a compact controller, diffused addressable RGB lighting, miniature wing actuator, and power/communications module, subject to feasibility testing.
- **Movement:** subtle wing flutter for a newly observed event; optional attention patterns for higher-priority states. User-controlled quiet mode, motion-off mode, brightness, and reduced-motion accessibility.
- **Size:** smaller and less bulky than the historical inspiration; exact dimensions TBD through component and mounting tests. A detachable, padded, non-damaging monitor mount must avoid pressure on display panels; freestanding option desirable.
- **USB-C:** proposed for power; local wired status signalling preferred for initial prototype. ESP32-S3 is a candidate, not a finalized board choice.
- **Status contract:** Teal All Good (recent checks found no outstanding findings), Indigo Heads Up, Purple Investigating, Amber Action Needed, Red Urgent, plus neutral/unavailable when sources are stale or disconnected. No colour-only alerts.
- **Privacy boundary:** hardware receives only a small status code, freshness/connection indication and optional motion pattern. No account IDs, IP addresses, credentials, logs or investigation evidence stored or sent to the device.

## Modular system
```text
Authorized imported data / supported provider integration
  -> evidence-aware comparison and review engine
  -> tablet/desktop application
  -> local status-only protocol
  -> interchangeable original Fairy / Desktop Dude shells
       [shared controller + lights + optional wing actuator]
```

## Milestone mapping
- **M1:** software status engine and interactive fictional dashboard.
- **M2:** source-linked review and account history, privacy controls.
- **M3:** mechanical/electrical feasibility, simulator, interchangeable shells and physical prototype.

## IP and originality checklist (not legal clearance)
- Preserve i-Buddy/MSN references only as historical inspiration, not product naming, marketing artwork or a claim of association.
- Draw original character concepts from scratch; independently engineer the enclosure, wing mechanism and mounting system.
- Do not copy original firmware, drivers, icons, logos, CAD files or proprietary PCB layouts.
- Before public release or commercial production, search relevant patents, industrial designs and trademarks in target markets, document independent design, and seek qualified IP review.
- Keep unverified dimensions, components and mechanisms labelled as hypotheses.
